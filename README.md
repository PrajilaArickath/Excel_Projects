# Retail Sales & Profitability Analysis (Excel)

An Excel analysis of Q1 2026 retail sales for an electronics and accessories business. It looks at one question: **where does the revenue come from, and why is so little of it kept as profit?**

![Dashboard](Sales%20Analysis%20%26%20Profitability-screenshot.png)

📁 Workbook: [Retail_Sales Analysis_Profitability_Project.xlsx](Retail_Sales%20Analysis_Profitability_Project.xlsx)

## Dataset
- 41 orders, 8 products, 3 categories (Computers, Electronics, Accessories) and 4 regions (North, South, East, West)
- Period: 1 January to 31 March 2026
- Currency: AED

## Headline numbers
| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Total revenue | AED 72,140 |
| Total cost | AED 51,726 |
| Gross profit | AED 20,414 |
| Gross profit margin | 28.3% |

## Key findings
1. **One product drives the business.** Laptops bring in 66% of revenue (AED 47,600) but earn only a 21.4% margin, so they produce 50% of gross profit.
2. **The best margins are on the smallest sales.** Accessories earn a 50.6% margin but are only 6.8% of revenue. Electronics sit in between at 39.4%.
3. **Sales fell sharply after January.** Revenue dropped from AED 36,110 in January to AED 17,880 in February (-50%) and AED 18,150 in March. February had only 4 orders, compared with 24 in January and 12 in March, so the fall came from fewer orders, not smaller ones.
4. **Margin follows the product mix.** Gross margin was 30.6% in January, 23.0% in February and 28.9% in March.
5. **North is the largest region, East the smallest.** North brought in AED 26,210 (36%) and West AED 22,520 (31%). East brought in only AED 3,780 (5%), but at the highest margin (38.9%). West had the lowest margin (24.7%).
6. **Wireless Mouse sold only 2 units.** Headphones sold the most, with 23 units.

## Recommendations
- **Protect laptop profit.** Review supplier cost and pricing, because a small margin gain on the biggest product has the largest effect on total profit.
- **Grow high-margin items.** Bundle accessories (keyboards, hubs, stands) with laptop sales to lift the overall margin.
- **Find out why February was weak.** Look at order counts, customer activity and promotions rather than only revenue.
- **Look at the East region.** It has strong margins but very low sales volume.
- **Review Wireless Mouse.** Check pricing, visibility and promotions, or decide whether to keep stocking it.
- **Track margin monthly, not only revenue.**

## Tools and skills used
Excel: formulas, PivotTables, slicers, charts and a KPI dashboard. Skills shown: data cleaning, profitability analysis, trend analysis and business reporting.

## Limitations
Margins here are **gross** margins (revenue minus product cost). The data has no operating expenses such as rent, salaries or marketing, so it cannot show whether the business is profitable overall. The dataset is also small (41 orders over 3 months), so the findings are indicative, not conclusive.
